# Professional Summary Generator

## Overview

Use this skill to create short professional summaries that match a target role and signal fit quickly.

## Workflow

1. Extract the target role, seniority, domain, years of experience, and strongest differentiators.
2. Identify proof points that can be stated in one line, such as scale, scope, leadership, technical depth, or business outcomes.
3. Draft multiple summary variants with different positioning angles if the user has not chosen one yet.
4. Keep the tone direct, specific, and evidence-based.
5. Tune the final version for resume use, LinkedIn use, or portfolio use depending on context.

## Output Format

- `Core positioning`: role target and differentiators
- `Summary variants`: usually 2 to 3 options
- `Recommended version`: the strongest fit for the target role
- `Optional headline`: a shorter title line if useful

## Guardrails

- Do not use first-person pronouns.
- Avoid generic filler such as `results-driven`, `hard-working`, or `team player` unless followed by proof.
- Do not turn the summary into an objective statement.
- Keep resume summaries tight, usually 2 to 4 lines.
- Never use the em dash character (—). Use a regular hyphen (-) instead.
