# Resume Template Mapper

## Overview

Use this skill to move resume content from one structure into another without losing critical evidence or breaking section logic.

## Workflow

1. Read the source resume content and the target template structure.
2. Create a section-to-section mapping before rewriting any content.
3. Identify information that does not fit cleanly and decide whether to merge, trim, or relocate it.
4. Rewrite bullets and headings only as much as needed to fit the target format.
5. Call out unsupported or overloaded sections early.

## Output Format

- `Mapping table`: source section to target section
- `Content moves`: what was merged, split, dropped, or renamed
- `Transformed copy`: ready-to-paste content for the target template
- `Template risks`: missing fields, overcrowded sections, or hidden assumptions

## Guardrails

- Preserve chronology and factual accuracy.
- Do not drop high-signal content just to keep symmetry across sections.
- When space is limited, cut repetition before cutting proof.
- If the target template is weak for ATS, call that out explicitly.
- Never use the em dash character (—). Use a regular hyphen (-) instead.
